# Brotato Co-op Weapons and Items Trading

In the co-op shop, give an item or weapon to another player for 10% (customizable with mod options) of its price. Weapons and items cannot be given to players with: no space, duplicate unique, items limit. 

Some items are excluded due to avoid abuse: builder turret (all variants), evolved leap (mecha), evolved leap (mutated), handcuffs, knot, shackles, coupon, used versions of items.
